## 2. What Glowtrace is, and is not

Glowtrace is a cosmetic self-care tool. It analyses photos of skin, hair and nails and
gives **wellness indicators**, routine suggestions and, for makeup artists, client
preparation tools.

**Glowtrace is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.** It does not
diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or condition. Indicators are estimates
affected by lighting, camera quality and angle. Do not use Glowtrace to make health
decisions. If you are worried about your skin, hair or nails, including a mole or mark that
changes, see a doctor or dermatologist. In an emergency, contact emergency services.

Product and DIY suggestions are general. Patch-test first, check ingredients against your
own allergies, and stop if irritation occurs.
